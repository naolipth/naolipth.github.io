The paper asks a simple question: Does higher inflation make future inflation harder to predict, and does that uncertainty itself contribute to higher inflation?

Our main finding is that **long-term uncertainty matters more than short-term volatility**. Once we allow long-term uncertainty to change over time, there is little evidence that a rise in inflation by itself makes inflation significantly more unpredictable in the short run.

The practical message for central banks is therefore to **focus less on reacting aggressively to every temporary price spike and more on keeping longer-term inflation expectations stable**.
